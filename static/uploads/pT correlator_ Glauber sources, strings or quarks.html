pT correlator: Glauber sources, strings or quarks

Source number comes from Glauber N_part and N_coll. A switch turns wounded nucleons into either 3 constituent quarks or 2 strings per collision. Fit to both centralities at once.

Data

Columns: √sNN, √C_pT/⟨pT⟩ in %, error, class (0 = 0-5%, 1 = 30-40%).

Top: posterior predictive means against the data. Middle and bottom: sources per wounded nucleon and hadrons per source in the switch model at the posterior mean E_tr and w.

Model comparison

ln BF is against constituent quarks without a switch. a is the number of initial sources per participant. Priors: a log-uniform 0.3-4, E_tr log-uniform 2.5-50 GeV, w uniform 0.5-10 GeV.

Model and inputs 
Glauber    N_part(E), N_coll(E) for 0-5% and 30-40%
           Monte Carlo Au+Au, Woods-Saxon, b classes,
           σ_NN from 24 mb (3 GeV) to 42 mb (200 GeV)
Sources    N_s = a N_part f_eff,  f_eff = 1 + (f-1) β(E)
           β = 1/(1+exp(-(E-E_tr)/w))
           quarks:  f = 3
           strings: f = s N_coll / N_part
pT         q ~ Gamma(k, mean 1/f_eff): total stopped pT fixed
Splitting  n̄_x(E) = max(1, c0 + c1 ln(E/10))  hadrons per
           quark or per string
           total hadrons per participant = f n̄_x
           n̄ = max(1, f n̄_x / f_eff)  hadrons per source
           n = 1 + Poisson(n̄-1), collinear
Window     α(E) = Δy / (2 y_beam)
C          = ⟨Σ_{i≠j} δp_i δp_j⟩ / ⟨N(N-1)⟩, plotted as √C/⟨pT⟩

The Glauber numbers are my own quick Monte Carlo with a minimum-distance cut omitted and impact-parameter centrality classes, so they are approximate (for example 0-5%: N_part 328 at 3 GeV, 352 at 200 GeV; 30-40%: 92 to 112). The σ_NN values are rough interpolations. N_coll(E) follows σ_NN. The model has no flow, no temperature or volume fluctuations, no string fusion, and the fit treats errors as independent and Gaussian.
