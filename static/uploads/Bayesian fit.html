Method

Per energy, α = ⟨p−p̄⟩/B_s and B̄ = ⟨p̄⟩/α (paper Eqs. 28 and 30), with α_p = α and ω[B̄] = 1. Predictions use Eqs. 8-11 and 21-24 for baryon stopping, and the generating-function composition of Eqs. 13-19 for quark stopping, with detailed balance α₂₁ = α₁₂ α/(1-α) and binomial (ideal gas with conservation) input. The switch scenario mixes the two stopped-charge cumulants with β(E) = 1/(1+exp(-(E-E_tr)/w)).

Likelihood: independent Gaussians for ω, Sσ and κσ² at each energy, so correlations between observables and energies are ignored. Priors are selectable above (defaults: α₁₁ uniform on 0.05-1, E_tr uniform in ln E on 7.7-50 GeV, w uniform on 0.5-10 GeV). Fixed priors use the nearest grid point. Evidence is the prior-weighted likelihood summed on a 40 × 36 × 14 grid. The kurtosis weight multiplies the κσ² χ² term, so for weights other than 1 the evidence uses a tempered likelihood and Bayes factors are not strict.
