This software library is designed to calculate light cluster production from transport model simulations. The approach bypasses the complexities associated with the dynamical treatment of clusters within these simulations. Rather than tracking microscopic details—which would require modeling every possible reaction involving clusters and nuclei—the model relies on the thermalization of the fireball at the break-up stage to readily estimate cluster yields. As a result, the primary advantages of this approach are its computational speed, robustness, and simplicity. Furthermore, this method provides a stringent test for equilibration in heavy-ion collisions and enhances our understanding of dense nuclear matter, particularly by leveraging the high sensitivity of light clusters to the equation of state.
