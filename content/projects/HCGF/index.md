A software library for calculating light cluster production in heavy-ion collisions. It avoids the complexity of treating clusters dynamically in transport simulations: rather than tracking every possible reaction involving clusters and nuclei, it assumes the fireball thermalizes at break-up and estimates cluster yields from that state.

The main advantages are computational speed, robustness, and simplicity. The method also provides a stringent test of equilibration in heavy-ion collisions and, because light clusters are highly sensitive to the equation of state, improves our understanding of dense nuclear matter.
