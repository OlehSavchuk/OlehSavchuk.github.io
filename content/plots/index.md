### Bayesian of transverse momentum correlations
A simple model where a fragmenting source in transverse directions are supposed to split into several particles conserving each source transverse momentum. The correlations observed are driven by the number of sources and the number of particles they split into. This model reflect a strong correlation observed at low energies likely caused by inelastic reactions in 2 to 2 scatterings that generate pairs with the same transverse momentum, at higher energies these particles become resonances that undergo splittings. At the energy of around 4 GeV resonances form, or by the virtue of hadron-parton duality very heavy resonances, that split into many particles, as these happens one should observe a dip in the number of sources but increased number of splittings in order to reproduce the data.
